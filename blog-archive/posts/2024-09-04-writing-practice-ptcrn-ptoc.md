# Writing practice: PTCRN <= PTOC

As a continuation of my writing practice from [last time](2024-09-03-writing-practice-coupling-example.md), I wrote up the [PTCRN <= PTOC fact](2024-06-06-note-crn-ptc.md).

In accordance with my goals, there are many instances of messy formatting, unclear explanation, and inconsistent terminology, but it is clear of absolute blockers.

Two parts I did add at the last moment that are worth mentioning: the introduction and related work. My first draft didn’t have them, but, when thinking about it, I sympathized with the idea that [it should be blindingly easy to know the contribution](https://youtu.be/WP-FkUaOcOM?si=Q6Aiy8uW5tOV_HP0), so I made sure to put that in the introduction, messy as it is.

The related work section also felt necessary, since omitting any mention of APOC just felt like a huge omission, even though this paper doesn’t directly use APOC. (A reader can overcome bad formatting and unclear explanations, but they can’t overcome being completely unaware of other relevant material.)
