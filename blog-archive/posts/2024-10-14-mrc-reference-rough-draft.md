# MRC reference (rough draft)

[**Correction (2024-10-15):** The 2024-10-14 PDF had an error in the MRC algorithm because of name shadowing. This has been fixed by introducing an additional variable.]

Towards being able to move on from MRC to other problems, the ideal result is posting a preprint to arXiv, but I don’t yet have an arXiv-ready preprint, and I find it challenging to even rough draft a preprint. So I gave myself instead the task of writing something I could refer back to in the future. This surprisingly easily evolved into something preprint-like.

Here is the rough draft:

Attached PDF: https://masharpe.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/mrc_ref_rough_draft_2024_10_15.pdf (not archived; its LaTeX source is archived at ../tex/mrc_ref_2024_10_15/mrc_ref.tex)

Some key remaining points:

- None of the proofs have been drafted yet. They will go in the appendix.
- In the case of Proposition 4, the detailed effect on subcouplings, I do not even have a proof sketch, just ideas. (I do have a direct proof of Corollary 2, though.)
- The pseudoinverse in Proposition 5 was a last-minute idea (to avoid needing to assume irreducibility), so my confidence in its correctness is not high, and in any case there may be a clearer way to express the formula.
- I don’t have a good citation for the true origin of simultaneously maximal coupling.
