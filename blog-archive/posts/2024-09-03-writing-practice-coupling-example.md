# Writing practice: coupling example

I have been struggling with writing my results at the next level beyond a blog post. I attribute this largely to lack of experience. My skills are so weak that it is near-impossible to meet a quality bar I’m happy with.

Therefore, to gain writing experience, I have lowered my ambitions. The only needs I truly have are:

- The document must be LaTeX.
- The document must be “complete” (title, abstract, body, references).
- I must post the document publicly.

The lowered ambitions:

- The idea should be one I’ve already blogged.
- The idea doesn’t need to be original, as long as I make that clear.
- The idea should be as small as I can find on short notice. If the idea requires a lot of explanation, that makes it much harder to complete the document.
- The formatting, aside from being LaTeX, does not need to be good at all. If there’s a critical blocker, I can fix it, but everything else should be left as-is.
- The document doesn’t need to be short. Making it concise requires extra effort. The idea should be small enough that the document is naturally short.
