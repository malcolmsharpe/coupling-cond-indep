The formulas in my previous post can be more straightforwardly derived using gradient, Hessian, and multivariate Taylor’s theorem. (I blame rustiness for not doing it this way in the first place.) Fixing p and letting q vary, here are the relevant equations:

F(q) := sum_i p_i (ln p_i - ln q_i)
grad F(q) = (-p_i/q_i)_i
grad F(p) = (-1)_i
grad^2 F(q) = Diag[ (p_i/q_i^2)_i ]
grad^2 F(p) = Diag[ (1/p_i)_i ]

G(q) := sum_i p_i (ln p_i - ln q_i)^2
grad G(q) = (-2 p_i/q_i (ln p_i - ln q_i))_i
grad G(p) = 0
grad^2 G(q) = Diag[ (2 p_i/q_i^2 (ln p_i - ln q_i + 1))_i ]
grad^2 G(p) = Diag[ (2/p_i)_i ]
