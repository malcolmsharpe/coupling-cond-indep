## 1. Independent coordinates

Four independent biased bits on each side, P[X_i=1] = (0.8, 0.6, 0.7, 0.3), P[Y_i=1] = (0.4, 0.5, 0.2, 0.6). CPT: product of products, so the laminar family {x1},{x2},{x1,x2},{x3},{x4},{x3,x4},{x1..x4} is guaranteed (paper: theorem "independent coordinates"). Per-coordinate coupling is optimal on singletons only; whole-vector CMC is optimal on the whole vector only.

## 3. Two-step masked-diffusion toy

Four positions. Step 1 unmasks p1, p2 independently; step 2 unmasks p3, p4 conditionally independently given (p1, p2). CPT: chain(chain(product(p1, p2), p3), p4). Guaranteed sets: {p1},{p2},{p1,p2},{p1,p2,p3},{p1..p4}. By the paper's Bayesian-network theorem, {p3} and {p4} cannot both be added in general (they share the ancestors p1, p2).
